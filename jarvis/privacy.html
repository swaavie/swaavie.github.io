Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

This Privacy Policy covers Jarvis, a single-user personal assistant. Jarvis has one user: its owner.

What data is collected

  • The owner's own mobile number, which he enters himself to receive text messages from Jarvis.
  • The text messages exchanged between Jarvis and that number.
  • With the owner's consent, the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar and Drive).

How it is used

  • The mobile number and messages are used only to deliver Jarvis's messages to the owner and to answer the messages he sends.
  • Google account data is used only to help that owner.
  • Data is stored on the owner's private devices.

Sharing

  • We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Data is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising.
  • Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Google access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Text messages stop when the owner replies STOP.
