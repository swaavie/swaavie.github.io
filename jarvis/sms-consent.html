By texting START you agree to receive recurring automated account notifications (order, shipment, inventory and sales alerts) from Jarvis, the operations assistant for an online retail business. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Who receives these messages

Jarvis is the operations assistant for an online retail store. It sends business notifications to the store's owner and staff. Today the only recipient is the business owner. There is no consumer sign-up, and Jarvis does not text customers.

How to opt in

  • Text START to +1 (850) 616-6790 from the mobile phone that should receive notifications, as shown above.
  • Notifications are sent only to a number that has texted START, and stop when that number replies STOP.

What the messages are

Automated account notifications for the business: new orders, shipments, inventory levels, sales alerts and a daily operations summary, plus replies to questions the recipient texts in. No marketing.

Terms

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with, sold to or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose. Message data is kept only to run the notifications. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
