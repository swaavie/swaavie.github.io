Jarvis SMS Consent

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Jarvis is a single-user personal assistant. Its text messages go to exactly one recipient: the account owner, at his own mobile number. There is no public sign-up, and no other phone number is ever enrolled or messaged.

How the owner opts in

  • The owner enables text messages himself, in his own private Jarvis dashboard, where he enters his own mobile number.
  • He then starts the conversation by texting the Jarvis number from that mobile himself.

What the messages are

Replies to questions the owner texts in, reminders, a daily summary of his own accounts and small online-selling business, and alerts he has set up himself. No marketing.

Terms

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with, sold to or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose. Message data is kept only to run the assistant. See the Jarvis Privacy Policy.
