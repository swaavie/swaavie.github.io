Jarvis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

  • Jarvis is a single-user personal app. It has one user: its owner.
  • With the owner's consent, Jarvis accesses only the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar and Drive), and only to help that owner.
  • Data is stored on the owner's private devices.
  • Data is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising.
  • Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
